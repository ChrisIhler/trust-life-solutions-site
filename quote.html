Common Questions

Frequently asked

Is this really free?

Yes. Our advisory, quoting, and application work is free to you. We’re compensated by the insurance carrier only if you choose to purchase a policy — and it doesn’t change the premium you pay. This is standard in independent insurance advisory and is why we can say there’s no cost, ever.

How is this different from Policygenius or Ethos?

Those are online lead generators that route you to a large call center and sell your info to multiple agents. We’re the opposite: one licensed advisor, one relationship, no call-center shuffle. Your information stays with us.

What carriers do you work with?

We have access to 20+ A-rated carriers including Mutual of Omaha, Foresters, Americo, Transamerica, F&G, Banner Life, SBLI, American Amicable, and more. Carrier availability varies by state and product.

Do I need a medical exam?

It depends on the product and carrier. Many policies today can be issued without a medical exam (called “simplified issue” or “accelerated underwriting”). Final Expense and most Mortgage Protection policies don’t require an exam. For larger face-amount Term or Whole Life policies, a simple exam may be required. We’ll know after the first conversation.

What if I already have a policy?

We offer free policy reviews. Many people are paying more than they need to, or have coverage that no longer fits their life. We’ll compare what you have to what’s available and tell you honestly whether it’s worth switching — even if the answer is no.

How fast can I get coverage?

For simplified-issue policies, coverage can sometimes be in place within a few days. Fully-underwritten Term or Whole Life policies typically take 2–6 weeks depending on the carrier and whether an exam is required.
