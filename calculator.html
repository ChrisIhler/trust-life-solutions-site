How the Math Works

The DIME method — explained simply.

DIME is the standard starting point financial advisors use to estimate life insurance need. It covers four categories of financial responsibility your income currently supports. Your family would need to replace those without you.

D

Debt

Everything you owe except the mortgage — credit cards, student loans, auto loans, medical debt. Your family shouldn't inherit these.

I

Income

Annual salary × years of support. Typically until the kids are out of the house, or until retirement savings would take over.

M

Mortgage

Your current mortgage balance. Sized to this, your family keeps the home free and clear.

E

Education

College costs for each child you want to fund. Typical public in-state: ~$100k per child. Private: $200k+.

Why we adjust for assets and existing coverage

Raw DIME tends to overstate the need. If you already have a $500k term policy through work and $200k in a brokerage account your spouse could access, that's $700k less in new insurance you need to buy. This calculator subtracts those to give you a realistic number.

What this doesn't include

This calculator doesn't adjust for Social Security survivor benefits (modest but real), inflation over a 20-year term, or the cost of hiring help to replace what a stay-at-home parent does. For a full picture, book a free consultation and we'll build a complete plan together.
